Using Jev for decisions

Choose openrouter:typesafe/jev-1.13 and put named questions in Params. Question types are noul (probability of yes), choice (named options), and score (ordered levels).

{"questions": {"refund": {"type": "noul", "instructions": "Is a refund requested?"}}}

Leave Output schema blank. Results contain answers keyed by question name, including probabilities. Jev accepts text and does not support tool grants, delegation, memory, learning, or human escalation.

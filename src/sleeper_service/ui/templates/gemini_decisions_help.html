Using Gemini Decisions

Choose google:gemini-decision-1 and put named questions in Params. Types are noul (probability of yes), choice (options in criteria), and score (ordered legend).

{"questions": {"refund": {"type": "noul", "instructions": "Is a refund requested?"}}}

Leave Output schema blank. Results contain answers keyed by question name. Gemini Decisions accepts text, images, audio, video, and PDFs, and does not support tool grants, delegation, memory, learning, or human escalation.

Uses a Google credential, GEMINI_API_KEY, or GOOGLE_API_KEY. Model documentation.

Using OpenAI Decisions

Choose openai:decisions/gpt-6-luna and put a list of questions in Params. Types are predicate (probability of yes), choice (options in choices), and score (ordered levels).

{"questions": [{"name": "refund", "type": "predicate", "instructions": "Is a refund requested?"}]}

Put task context in Prompt and leave Output schema blank. Results contain an answers list in question order; an answer may be a refusal. OpenAI Decisions accepts text and images, and does not support tool grants, delegation, memory, learning, or human escalation.

Uses an OpenAI credential or OPENAI_API_KEY. Question format and examples.
